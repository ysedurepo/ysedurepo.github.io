1. Biography#

Youssef Saadi is an Associate Professor at the Faculty of Sciences and Techniques of Beni Mellal (Sultan Moulay Slimane University) and a member of the Intelligence, Data and Computing Team (IDC Team). His work focuses on artificial intelligence applied to computer vision, deep learning and, more recently, large language models and agentic AI systems.

With more than 8 years of research experience in AI and over 20 peer-reviewed publications, he works to connect international AI research trends with Moroccan academic and industrial contexts. He also serves as a reporter and examiner for Master’s and doctoral theses, and as a reviewer for scientific conferences.

6. AI Projects#

Knee abnormality detection — RSNA 2026 (Kaggle)#

Computer vision pipeline for musculoskeletal imaging, addressing the weak supervision challenge at the heart of the dataset: label construction from multilingual radiology reports and grouped cross-validation by scanner fingerprint. Stack: PyTorch · DICOM · Weights & Biases

Video classification on UCF101#

Comparative study of the R3D, MC3, R(2+1)D and CNN-LSTM spatio-temporal architectures, with experiment tracking and training resumption. Stack: PyTorch · torchvision · W&B

Voice-driven Text-to-SQL agent#

Conversational agent converting spoken queries into SQL statements, built on an orchestrated agent architecture. Stack: LangGraph · LangChain · Whisper · SQL

Multi-source RAG chatbot#

Retrieval-augmented generation system with retrieval optimisation (MultiVectorRetriever, RAPTOR, ColBERT, HyDE) and an interactive interface. Stack: LangChain · FAISS · Ollama · Groq · Streamlit

Deep learning for medical imaging (DICOM)#

End-to-end DICOM preprocessing, augmentation and training pipeline for classification and segmentation models, including a synthetic data generator. Stack: PyTorch · pydicom · MONAI
