Probability Calibration in Machine Learning: A Practical Introduction

Probability calibration Probability calibration is a post-processing step in machine learning and statistics that consists of ensuring that the probabilities predicted by a model correspond to real-world realities. Why is this necessary? Many classification algorithms (such as support vector machines, random forests, or deep neural networks) produce scores or percentages, but these do not always reflect a true statistical probability. For example, imagine a medical diagnostic model that predicts a patient has a 90% chance of having a disease.
